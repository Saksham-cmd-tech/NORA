1. Introduction

#

NORA (Notification Organized Rail Assistant) is a privacy-focused train ticket wallet and journey companion designed for Indian Railway passengers.

Your privacy is a fundamental part of NORA's design. The application is built around an offline-first approach, keeping your train tickets and associated journey information on your device rather than transmitting them to our servers.

This Privacy Policy explains what information NORA accesses, how it is processed and stored, and the choices available to you when using the application.

By using NORA, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Access and Store

#

NORA may process the following information when you use its features.

2.1 Train Ticket and Journey Information

When you import or manually add a ticket, NORA may process information such as:

  • Passenger names and ticket details.
  • PNR numbers and booking status.
  • Train names, train numbers, and journey dates.
  • Boarding, destination, and intermediate stations.
  • Coach numbers, seat numbers, and travel class.
  • Arrival and departure times.
  • Fare details and reservation information.

This information is used to display, organize, and manage your saved journeys.

2.2 Booking Messages and Shared Content

NORA can process supported booking messages shared through your device's share sheet or detected through its clipboard feature.

When you choose to import a booking message, NORA extracts relevant ticket information locally on your device.

NORA does not require unrestricted SMS-reading access for the described import methods. Clipboard access is intended to help recognize supported booking information.

Clipboard Privacy Notice: Clipboard information is not intended to be transmitted to any external server. Avoid copying sensitive information into the clipboard unless you intend to import it into NORA.

2.3 Manually Entered Information

You may manually enter ticket and journey information. This information is stored locally so that you can access and manage your tickets.

2.4 Device and Application Information

NORA is designed not to collect analytics, advertising identifiers, or behavioral tracking information.

The application does not intentionally transmit personal ticket information, usage analytics, or device identifiers to a NORA-operated server.

Your device operating system may independently maintain system logs or diagnostic information according to its own settings and policies.

3. How Your Information Is Used

#

Information processed by NORA is used exclusively to provide its application features, including:

  • Saving and displaying train tickets.
  • Organizing upcoming and past journeys.
  • Recognizing supported booking messages.
  • Updating ticket details when new supported booking information is imported.
  • Displaying train routes, coach layouts, and seat information when the necessary data is available.
  • Scheduling departure reminders.
  • Sharing boarding passes when you explicitly request it.
  • Exporting your ticket wallet when you explicitly request an export.
  • Adding journey information to your device calendar when you request calendar integration.

NORA does not sell your personal information or use your ticket details for targeted advertising.

4. Local Storage and Data Security

#

NORA stores ticket and journey information in a local SQLite database using its configured database encryption mechanisms.

The application is designed to keep your saved ticket information on your device and does not require a NORA-operated cloud account or server for local wallet functionality.

We recommend keeping your device protected with a screen lock and installing operating system security updates.

Although reasonable technical measures are used to protect locally stored information, no method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Important: The level of encryption depends on the actual database configuration and encryption keys implemented in the installed application.

5. Network Access and Third-Party Services

#

NORA's local wallet functionality is designed to operate offline.

Certain optional actions, such as opening an external train-status service or accessing information supplied by a third-party service, may require an internet connection and may take you outside NORA.

When you open an external website or service, that provider may process information according to its own privacy policy. NORA does not control the privacy practices of external providers.

NORA does not claim affiliation with, or official endorsement by, Indian Railways, IRCTC, or CRIS unless explicitly stated otherwise.

Train information and third-party service availability may vary.

6. Notifications and Departure Reminders

#

If you enable departure reminders, NORA may schedule local notifications for upcoming journeys, including reminders approximately two hours and one hour before departure.

These reminders are intended to be generated on your device using the operating system's notification and scheduling capabilities.

NORA does not require a remote notification server for locally scheduled departure reminders.

You can disable reminders through the application's settings or your device's notification settings.

Actual delivery may depend on device permissions, battery optimization, operating system restrictions, and alarm scheduling behavior.

7. Calendar, Sharing, and Export

#

NORA may allow you to:

  • Add journey information to your device calendar.
  • Share a boarding pass or ticket summary through your device's sharing interface.
  • Export your ticket wallet into a JSON file.

These actions occur when you explicitly choose the relevant feature.

Once information is shared with another application, exported to a file, or added to an external service, its subsequent handling is governed by the permissions and privacy practices of that application or service.

Tip: Exported files may contain sensitive ticket information. Store and share them carefully.

8. Permissions

#

NORA requests or uses device capabilities only when required for its features.

Depending on your installed version and the features you use, these may include:

  • Notifications and alarms: To schedule and display journey reminders.
  • Clipboard access: To recognize supported ticket information when you open or refresh the application.
  • Share sheet integration: To receive booking messages that you explicitly share with NORA.
  • Calendar access: If required by the implemented calendar integration.
  • Internet access: If required for optional external services or train information.

NORA's described ticket-import approach does not require unrestricted SMS-reading permission or continuous background notification-listener access.

You can manage applicable permissions through your device settings. Disabling a permission may prevent its associated feature from functioning.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

#

Your saved tickets and associated journey information remain on your device until you delete them or remove the application's stored data.

NORA provides an option to delete saved tickets from within the application, subject to the functionality available in your installed version.

Uninstalling NORA or clearing its application data may remove locally stored information. However, exported files, shared copies, calendar entries, and information independently retained by other applications may remain.

NORA does not maintain a central server-side ticket database for the described local-storage functionality, so there is no corresponding server-side ticket record to request deletion of.

Please export any information you wish to retain before deleting your wallet or uninstalling the application.

10. Children's Privacy

#

NORA is a general-purpose travel utility and is not specifically directed at children.

NORA does not intentionally collect personal information from children through a NORA-operated server. Ticket information entered into the application is handled according to the local-storage practices described in this policy.

Parents and guardians should supervise the handling and sharing of sensitive travel information on devices used by children.

This Privacy Policy may be updated when NORA introduces new features, changes its technical implementation, or needs to reflect applicable legal or platform requirements.

Updates will be published at the location where this policy is hosted, along with a revised effective date.

You should review this page periodically for changes.
